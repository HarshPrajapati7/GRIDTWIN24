# Founder decision memo

## Decision

Proceed with GRIDTWIN-24 as a research prototype on the official **Tomorrow's Load** problem statement.

## Why this path

It offers a coherent path from the exact challenge deliverable to a more complete physical system: forecast -> scenario -> approved action -> measured response. It is useful in a domain that affects households and businesses, has an understandable paying customer (facilities operators), and supports a tangible hardware demonstration without requiring hazardous mains experimentation.

## Why not the other tracks for this build

- Chulha to Cylinder: important social problem, but the supplied brief is principally survey classification. Sensor hardware could become a separate indoor-air-quality product and risk distracting from the required model.
- Ward Pulse: serious civic application, but geospatial validation, under-reporting bias and public procurement make it harder to establish end-to-end value in a short build.
- Soy Leaf Doctor: a practical farmer-facing product, but existing public crop-diagnostic systems reduce differentiation and the supplied task is a narrow image classifier.
- Report Navigator: Gemma multimodality is a natural fit, but general document Q&A is crowded. A specialised numerical evidence engine could be valuable, but it has no strong reason to use hardware and less physical differentiation.

## Non-negotiable product truth

The team's first milestone is the official 24-hour demand forecast using the organiser's data. The site-level scheduler and HIL bench are explicitly extensions. No claims about actual savings or grid-scale control are permitted until measured in a real, appropriately supervised pilot.
