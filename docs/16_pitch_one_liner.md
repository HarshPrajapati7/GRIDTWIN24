# Pitch one-liner and 30-second pitch

**One-liner:** GRIDTWIN-24 turns tomorrow's electricity-demand forecast into a testable operating plan — and includes a guarded 12-volt DC energy-bench design for validating the sensing and control path.

**30-second pitch:** Electricity demand forecasting usually ends with a line chart. We are building the layer after the chart. GRIDTWIN-24 predicts the next 24 hours, identifies high-demand windows, lets a facility operator test load-shifting scenarios against tariff and capacity constraints, and includes a low-voltage hardware-in-the-loop design to test the sensing and control workflow after assembly and validation. Gemma 4 acts as the operator copilot, but the forecast, optimisation and safety logic remain deterministic and auditable. We start with campuses and hostels, where a facilities manager can test the value on real interval-meter data.
